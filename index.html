❔ Welcome to Emberfall

Move & explore
W A S D walk · Shift sprint · right-drag rotate camera · scroll zoom · F free-roam camera (then Space/Shift fly up/down).

Talk & quests
Walk up to Arin or any villager and press E to talk — they remember you, form opinions, and adapt to how you speak. Ask a villager for a task to start a hunt quest.
🎤 Talk by voice: click the mic in the dialogue box, or switch on 🗣 Hands-free and just speak — each NPC answers aloud in their own voice, which changes with their mood (best in Chrome/Edge).

Fight
Space / left-click attack · 1 Power Strike · 2 Whirlwind · 3 Heal · Q drink potion. Kills drop gold & potions — spend gold at a market stall (T). Each town's market trades its own goods — buy where they're made, sell where they're scarce; L opens your trade ledger. Take a rowing boat at a landing (E) and steer it like a horse — beware the water beasts. V switches to first-person view.

Rest
R rest at the inn · Z sleep in a bed to pass the night.
E also opens doors and lights or puts out campfires (a lit fire slowly heals you). Smash 📦 crates with attacks for loot.

A living world
Villagers keep a daily schedule (work → tavern → bed) and gossip about you. Watch for world events (raids, a merchant, festivals, a wounded stranger) and weather that changes the fight. Two glowing ◈ seals outside the walls open a vault — hold both at once (bring a friend, or ask Kael with E).
G ping a spot · X emotes · M world map · 🎵 music · 📊 session stats.
Travel — five more towns lie along the roads. Board a horse-drawn coach (press E beside it, E again to get off), or tame a wild horse in the meadows (press E beside it a few times), then H to mount/dismount — Shift to gallop. Far from your horse? H whistles it over.
